# LearnStudy

### Transform your YouTube playlists into structured, distraction-free study courses.

---

## 📖 About LearnStudy

**LearnStudy** is a high-performance, distraction-free learning platform engineered to help students and professionals study YouTube lectures in a clean, focused environment. 

By stripping away the algorithmic recommendations, comments, and unrelated video rabbit holes of standard YouTube, LearnStudy transforms raw playlists into well-organized interactive curriculums. Integrated with locally-run smart AI tools, students can summarize complex material, test their comprehension with auto-generated quizzes, bookmark key timestamps, and maintain deep focus using a built-in productivity suite.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Playlist & Video Importers**: Seamlessly import any public YouTube playlist or individual video link into a personalized learning module.
*   **Aesthetic Distraction-Free Player**: Learn in an elegant, modern interface designed to keep your focus strictly on the educational content.
*   **Continuous Learning Trackers**:
    *   *Resume Playback*: Automatically remembers and resumes from your last-watched timestamp down to the exact second.
    *   *Continue Watching*: Quickly pick up right where you left off from your home dashboard.
    *   *Watch History*: Detailed logs of all previously watched lecture sessions.
*   **Personalized Playlist Library**: Curate, filter, and organize your favorite courses into an easily accessible workspace.
*   **Comprehensive Study Toolkit**:
    *   *Dynamic Progress Tracking*: Automatic calculation of course-completion percentages and module completion marks.
    *   *Interactive Study Notes*: Write rich-text lecture notes bound to specific courses.
    *   *Smart Bookmarks*: Drop anchors at key moments in any video to return to them instantly.
    *   *Course-Wide Search*: Instantly locate specific lectures across your imported library.
*   **Personal AI Study Assistant**:
    *   *Auto-Summarization*: Generate comprehensive text summaries of dense lectures.
    *   *Doubt Solver*: Highlight confusing concepts or pose specific questions directly to the AI.
    *   *AI Quiz & Flashcards*: Challenge yourself with dynamic multi-topic quizzes and interactive active-recall flashcards generated directly from course content.
*   **Deep Work Productivity Hub**:
    *   *Integrated Pomodoro Timer*: Customizable study-break intervals to optimize your cognitive retention.
    *   *Advanced Study Statistics*: Visualize your total learning hours, streak counts, and progress trends over time.
*   **Flawless Performance & Portability**:
    *   *Full Offline State Preservation*: Automatically stores and saves all notes, history, progress, and settings to your local browser storage.
    *   *Aesthetic Dark Theme*: Beautifully crafted high-contrast dark visual identity for comfortable night study.
    *   *Fully Responsive Layout*: Desktop-optimized layout with a fluid, mobile-first design for studying on any screen size.

## 🎯 Why LearnStudy?

Traditional video platforms are built to maximize engagement through endless distractions. LearnStudy is built to maximize **comprehension**.

*   **No YouTube Distractions**: Eliminates recommendations, ads, sidebar feeds, and community comments.
*   **Better Focus**: Keeps you in a locked-in state with specialized learning modules and built-in Pomodoro cycles.
*   **Organized Learning**: Groups video materials logically, presenting them as a structured curriculum with explicit progress milestones.
*   **Faster Revision**: Quickly search your notes, refer to your timestamped bookmarks, and review custom-tailored AI flashcards.
*   **Better Productivity**: Monitors your visual learning metrics and streak histories to keep your momentum going.

## 🖥️ Screens Overview

*   **Home Dashboard**: Your centralized learning center displaying your daily streaks, weekly learning hour graphs, current active courses, and a quick-resume carousel.
*   **Study Player**: An immersive dual-pane interface with the interactive YouTube video on the left, and your dynamic workspace (Notes, Bookmarks, and AI Assistant panel) on the right.

## 🧠 Smart AI Features

LearnStudy integrates with **Google Gemini AI** to act as a highly-trained personal tutor. To enable these features, users can easily input their own **Google Gemini API Key** inside the Settings Panel.

*   **AI Lecture Summaries**: Get structured, bulleted summaries of long videos in seconds.
*   **AI Quiz Generation**: Instantly construct interactive, multi-choice tests to evaluate your active recall.
*   **Flashcards**: Automatically create smart decks focusing on core terms, vocabulary, and formulas.
*   **Concept Explanation & Doubt Solving**: Simply highlight a term or ask a direct question to receive plain-English breakdowns.

> 🔒 **Privacy-First Security**: Your API keys are strictly saved and processed locally inside your browser's secure `localStorage`. Your key is never shared, sent, or processed by any external third-party server.

## ⚡ Performance & Design

*   **Ultralight Loading**: Built to load immediately, with fast-path rendering and instant client-side transitions.
*   **Optimized IFrame Handling**: Employs deep integration with the YouTube IFrame Player API to control playback smoothly.
*   **Progressive Playlist Ingestion**: Dynamically loads massive playlists progressively, allowing you to start studying immediate chunks without waiting for lengthy imports.
*   **Local Caching Engine**: Minimizes API request overhead by strategically caching playlist structures and metadata.

## 🛡️ Privacy Policy

*   **No Login Required**: Start studying instantly. There are no registration forms, usernames, or passwords.
*   **100% Local Data**: All of your watch histories, notes, bookmarks, streaks, and study stats are kept completely private and stored directly in your browser.
